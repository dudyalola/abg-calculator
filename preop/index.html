RCRI Cardiac — MACE

Revised Cardiac Risk Index. Estimates cardiac complications after noncardiac surgery. One point each.

RCRI score 0

Class I–IV per the original 1999 Lee classification (I = 0, II = 1, III = 2, IV = ≥3). The percentages are the updated pooled estimates from the 2017 CCS Perioperative Guidelines, which supersede the 1999 Lee figures (0.4% / 0.9% / 6.6% / ≥11%) for the same four classes. Source: MDCalc.

ARISCAT Pulmonary — PPC

Predicts postoperative pulmonary complications, including respiratory failure, after major non-cardiac surgery. Not validated for cardiac or thoracic surgery.

ARISCAT score —

Low <26 (~1.6% PPC) · Intermediate 26–44 (~13.3%) · High ≥45 (~42.1%). Age scores 0 at ≤50, 3 at 51–80, 16 above 80; SpO₂ scores 0 at ≥96%, 8 at 91–95%, 24 at ≤90%. Best calibrated in Western European populations — use caution extrapolating. Pulmonary risk only; evaluate cardiovascular, bleeding and procedure-specific risk separately. Source: MDCalc.
