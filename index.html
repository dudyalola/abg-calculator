VBG as a surrogate

pH and HCO3 track well: venous pH runs 0.03–0.04 lower and venous HCO3 1–2 meq/L higher than arterial.

pCO2 and pO2 vary too much to convert, especially in shock or heart failure — those still need an arterial stick.

A normal venous pCO2 has a high negative predictive value for CO2 retention, so it works as a rule-out for type II respiratory failure.
